Signs Your AC Needs Service

If any of these sound familiar, don't wait for a total breakdown — small problems grow fast in Houston heat.

  • Blowing warm or room-temperature air
  • AC runs constantly but the house stays hot
  • Weak airflow from the vents
  • Strange noises — grinding, squealing, banging
  • Ice on the lines or water leaking around the unit
  • Thermostat issues or the system won't turn on
  • Unusual smells when the AC kicks on
  • Electric bill climbing with no explanation
